About the Artist
Kesarbai Kerkar was one of the foremost classical vocalists of the 20th century, known for her powerful voice and authoritative renditions of traditional Indian music. Her recordings preserve a repertoire that influenced later generations of classical and popular musicians.
About the Track
“Jaat Kahan Ho” is a traditional composition presented here in a classical interpretation that highlights vocal ornamentation and modal phrasing. The performance emphasizes expressive timing and nuanced melodic development.
Voyager Golden Record
The Voyager Golden Record was included aboard NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft launched in 1977. It contains a selection of music, sounds, and images meant to represent the diversity of life and culture on Earth. This selection places the track in a broader cultural context as part of humanity's attempt to communicate across the cosmos.
