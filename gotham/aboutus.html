About Us

Gotham is a privately-operated digital observatory based on secure, isolated servers in Iceland. Our focus is on maintaining a cleaner internet by scanning, tracing, and eliminating threats that compromise digital integrity.

What We Do

  • Detect and remove malware, spyware, and injection-based attacks.
  • Identify and report online fraud, blackmail operations, and financial traps.
  • Trace digital patterns related to terrorism, cyber grooming, and radicalization.
  • Flag manipulation systems including fake propaganda, AI-generated misinformation, and deepfake abuse.
  • Monitor dark-net forums, encrypted platforms, and suspicious network traffic.
  • Preserve access to privacy-respecting tools, open-source intelligence, and uncensored content.

Infrastructure

  • Self-hosted servers with full disk encryption, physically located in Iceland.
  • Zero third-party dependencies — no cookies, no trackers, no cloud APIs.
  • Daily autonomous scans and manual audits for threat isolation.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no commercial data capture.

Policy

Gotham does not operate under sponsorships or corporate funding. All activities are user-powered and community-driven.
